# LeakAudit — Founder Beta 20 Outreach Playbook

Three outreach templates for recruiting the first 10–20 Founder Beta merchants. Every
claim in these templates is grounded in what LeakAudit actually does today (see
`RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` and `about.tsx` for the exact formulas) — nothing here promises a
capability that isn't shipped.

A channel-risk note up front, since two of these three channels carry real risk of
getting an account flagged or banned if used carelessly: Reddit's site-wide rules (and
nearly every subreddit's own rules) treat unsolicited self-promotional DMs as spam,
independent of how genuine the message is — a cold DM into someone's inbox who never
engaged with you first is a bannable pattern on many accounts, not just an etiquette
issue. The safer version of the Reddit play below is to use this voice in genuine
comment replies on threads where someone is already complaining about profit-tracking
apps or hidden fees, and only DM people who reply positively to that comment — not to
mass-DM strangers cold. Cold email to store owners also has real compliance exposure
depending on how the recipient list was sourced (CAN-SPAM in the US, GDPR/PECR if any
recipients are in the EU/UK) — this template assumes a legitimately sourced,
opt-in-adjacent list (e.g., people who signed up for updates, or a scraped list where
you've confirmed applicable consent/legitimate-interest rules), not a cold-scraped list
with no basis for contact.

---

## 1. Reddit / Community Direct Message

Use as a *reply-first* message to someone who's already posted about profit-tracking
app frustration, hidden payment fees, or app bloat — not as a cold DM to a stranger.

```
Subject: (n/a — DM, no subject line)

Hey — saw your post about [specific pain: e.g. "BeProfit's pricing cliff" / "getting
blindsided by FX fees"]. I'm building something in exactly that space and figured I'd
just ask instead of guessing.

I'm a solo dev, not a marketing account — built LeakAudit because I got tired of every
"profit tracker" app either being a $99/mo black box or taking a 2-hour spreadsheet
setup before it tells you anything. It scans your store the second you install it — no
CSV uploads — and flags four specific things: FX/payment fee drag, leftover app scripts
still running in your theme, SKUs actually losing you money, and a rising return rate.
Read-only the whole way, never touches your store.

I'm giving away lifetime free access to the first 20 people who'll actually use it and
tell me honestly what's wrong with it — no credit card, no trial clock. If that's not
your thing no worries at all, just wanted to flag it since it sounded relevant. If it
is: [link]
```

## 2. Cold Email to Shopify Store Owners (3 sentences max)

```
Subject: Quick question about your Shopify margins

Hi [First Name] — quick one: have you checked lately how much FX fees and leftover app
scripts might be quietly eating into [Store Name]'s margins?

I built a free tool that scans your store in 60 seconds (read-only, no CSV uploads) and
shows exactly where that's happening — no credit card required, just an honest look at
the numbers.

If you're curious, here's the link: [https://leakaudit-app.fly.dev/] — happy to answer
anything directly if you'd rather just ask.
```

## 3. LinkedIn / Twitter (X) DM

Targeting bootstrapped DTC/e-commerce founders specifically (not agencies or large
brands — the message assumes a solo or small-team operator who's price- and time-
sensitive).

```
Hey [Name] — following your build-in-public journey with [Store/Brand]. Quick one: I
built a free Shopify app that scans your store in 60 seconds and flags exactly where
you're losing margin to FX fees, dead app scripts, underpriced SKUs, or return drift —
no setup, no CC. Giving free lifetime access to the first 20 founders willing to try it
and tell me straight what's broken. Worth a look? [link]
```

## Notes for whoever sends these

- Swap in the actual specific pain point / post content before sending #1 — a generic
  version of that template reads exactly like the spam it's trying not to be.
- None of these promise a specific dollar figure up front — the real numbers only show
  up after a scan, and that's deliberate: overpromising a number before someone's data
  is in front of you is the fastest way to erode the "founder-to-founder, not a
  marketing account" tone all three are going for.
- Track response/install rate per channel from day one — with only 20 slots, it's worth
  knowing quickly which channel is actually converting rather than spreading effort
  evenly across all three by default.
